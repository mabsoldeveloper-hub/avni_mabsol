Terms & Conditions and Data Privacy

Mabsol CRM Data Synchronization Protocol & Security Agreement

📁

We Are Only Accessing Your Files Data

The Mabsol CRM Desktop Agent exclusively reads local ERP / accounting database files (.dbf, .fpt, .cdx) located in your designated folder to calculate inventory, stock statements, sales, purchases, and ledger transactions for your private CRM dashboard.

🛡️

Not Collecting or Using Your Personal Details

We are NOT collecting or using your personal details. The desktop agent does NOT inspect, scan, or harvest your personal files, browsing history, private identity documents, or non-business files. Only authorized ERP database tables are read.

🔑

Not Using Your License (Dedicated & Protected)

We are NOT using or sharing your license key. Your license key is cryptographically assigned exclusively to your authorized company and verified workstation. It is never reused, leased, or accessible by third parties.

🔒

We Keep Private Your Data

We keep private your data at all times. All data synchronized from your local system to your cloud workspace is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption. Your data is isolated strictly to your organization and is never shared, marketed, or sold.

💻

Desktop Account Verification & Security

To safeguard your business analytics, sign-in access on the desktop application requires multi-factor account verification (OTP). Each computer is independently authenticated to prevent unauthorized access.
